The 60-Point Google Ads Audit Checklist

Work in three passes, not one sitting: money leaks first (tracking), then money flow (search terms, budgets, bidding), then the compounders (ads, Quality Score, landing pages). Twenty minutes a pass per account.

Pass 1 — Tracking & conversions (the money leaks) · 18 points

Setup fundamentals

  1. 1Auto-tagging is ON — Admin → Account settings. Off means no GCLID: conversions report as direct/organic and Smart Bidding flies blind.
  2. 2Conversion actions exist at all — Goals → Conversions. No actions = every other number in the account is decoration.
  3. 3The right actions are marked Primary — Primary actions are what Smart Bidding optimises to. Check the highest-intent actions (Purchase, Lead) are Primary, not buried as Secondary.
  4. 4A biddable goal is set — Goals table: at least one goal category biddable, or campaigns have no account-default target.
  5. 5Campaign-level goal overrides are intentional — any campaign using a custom conversion subset instead of account defaults: was that deliberate, or legacy config nobody remembers?

Double-counting traps

  1. 6No duplicate Primary actions in the same category — two Primary "Purchase" actions = every sale counted twice = ROAS fiction.
  2. 7No similar-named near-duplicates — "Form Submit (GTM)" + "Form Submission (GA4)" both Primary usually track the same event twice.
  3. 8Lead subcategories don't overlap — Lead + Submit Form + Request Quote all Primary often fire on one form.
  4. 9Purchase counting mode suits the business — "Every" vs "One" — check repeat-purchase logic is a decision, not a default.
  5. 10Micro-conversions aren't Primary — Page View or Engaged Visit as Primary teaches Smart Bidding to buy cheap curiosity.

Is it actually firing?

  1. 11Every Primary webpage tag has fired in the last 365 days — a tag with a year of silence is uninstalled or blocked, not unlucky.
  2. 12No tag has gone quiet recently — fired 31–90 days ago but zero in the last 30 = something broke in a site update or consent change.
  3. 13Non-webpage actions (calls, imports) show recent activity.
  4. 14Smart Bidding campaigns have converting Primary actions — tCPA on a campaign whose conversions never fire is autopilot with no compass.

Match tracking to the business model

  1. 15Ecommerce: a Purchase action with revenue values exists.
  2. 16Ecommerce: funnel events (Add to Cart, Begin Checkout) tracked as secondary signals — they speed up learning on low-volume accounts.
  3. 17Lead gen: at least one real lead conversion (form, quote, booking).
  4. 18Lead gen: secondary touchpoints tracked — calls, email clicks, chat — so the account sees the whole enquiry picture.

Pass 2 — Money flow (search terms, budgets, bidding) · 21 points

Search terms (30 days unless stated)

  1. 19Spend on non-converting terms — terms with meaningful spend and zero conversions: negative the clearly irrelevant today.
  2. 20Dead terms over 365 days — a year of spend with nothing back has had its chance; negative immediately.
  3. 21Converting terms that aren't keywords — 3+ conversions from a term you never added: add as exact match and take bid control.
  4. 22Cannibalisation — the same term matching in 3+ campaigns means you're bidding against yourself; funnel each term to one campaign with campaign-level negatives.
  5. 23High-impression, near-zero-CTR terms — hundreds of impressions at under 0.5% CTR drags Quality Score: negative or fix the ad.
  6. 24Terms converting at 3× your average CPA — negative, or quarantine in a campaign with its own target.
  7. 25Informational searches ("how to", "guide") — check 3-month data before negating: for service businesses these can be next month's clients. Decide, don't reflex.
  8. 26Competitor-brand searches — bidding on competitors is a strategy; paying for them by accident is a leak. Move to a dedicated campaign or negate.

Budgets & pacing

  1. 27Severe overspend pace — any campaign tracking 150%+ of its daily budget pace exhausts the month early. Catch it on the 10th, not in the month-end report.
  2. 28Moderate overspend pace (125–150%) — watch-list and adjust.
  3. 29Underspend & budget-limited mix — a campaign at half its budget isn't performing, it's coasting — while a winner loses impression share to budget. Reallocate.
  4. 30Seasonal calendar — know your peaks: budgets up 2–4 weeks BEFORE the peak; in dips, don't panic-spend — test creative instead.

Bid strategies

  1. 31Smart Bidding has enough data — tCPA/tROAS under ~30 conversions a month gives Google nothing to learn from; use Maximise Conversions until volume justifies a target.
  2. 32Manual CPC with real volume — 30+ conversions a month on manual bidding wastes signal you already paid for.
  3. 33Maximise Conversions without a target — meaningful spend with no CPA/ROAS constraint spends to the budget, not to profit.
  4. 34Enhanced CPC anywhere — deprecated; migrate.
  5. 35CPA target vs actual sanity — target 50% above actuals = Google overbids; 40% below = volume strangled. Targets should sit near reality, then move deliberately.
  6. 36ROAS target vs actual sanity — same check, other direction.

Bid adjustments (180-day windows)

  1. 37Device — mobile/desktop/tablet converting 30%+ apart deserves an adjustment.
  2. 38Day & hour — if Tuesdays convert and Sundays burn, schedule it.
  3. 39Age — meaningful conversion-rate gaps across age ranges.
  4. 40Gender — same test.
  5. 41Audiences — segments converting far above/below average: bid up the winners, trim the rest.

Pass 3 — The compounders (ads, Quality Score, landing pages) · 19 points

Ad coverage & health

  1. 42No ETA-only ad groups — Expanded Text Ads are sunset; Google can't even edit them. RSAs, now.
  2. 43No single-ad ad groups — one ad means no test running, which means no learning.
  3. 44No RSAs rated Poor — usually headlines too similar; give the machine variety to assemble.
  4. 45Push Average RSAs up — add genuinely different headlines: USPs, numbers, varied CTAs.
  5. 46No disapproved ads sitting unfixed.
  6. 47"Approved (limited)" ads reviewed — know what the restriction is and whether you can remove it.
  7. 48Enabled ads with zero impressions (30d) — disapproval or Ad Rank is keeping them out of the auction entirely.

Assets & PMax

  1. 49Sitelinks on every meaningful campaign — 4+ per campaign; free SERP real estate.
  2. 50Callouts everywhere — 4–6 short trust/benefit lines.
  3. 51PMax asset groups complete — missing required assets locks the campaign out of whole networks.
  4. 52No PMax assets rated Low — replace, don't accumulate.
  5. 53PMax has a real video — without one Google auto-generates something you would never sign off.

Quality Score (where the spend is)

  1. 54No QS ≤4 keywords carrying real spend — you're paying a surcharge on every click.
  2. 55QS 5–6 keywords with spend — the cheapest CPC savings in the account usually live here.
  3. 56"Below average" landing page experience flags — then actually test the page speed (Core Web Vitals), not just the score.
  4. 57"Below average" expected CTR flags — the ad, not the page: put the keyword in the headlines, sharpen the offer.
  5. 58"Below average" ad relevance flags — restructure: tighter ad groups, copy that addresses the exact theme.

Landing pages (the bit most audits skip)

  1. 59A visible call-to-action above the fold, with intent in the copy — "Get a Quote", not "Learn More".
  2. 60Form friction and trust — more than ~5 visible fields loses completions; and if there's no social proof anywhere above the fold (reviews, counts, guarantees), you're asking for a click on faith.
