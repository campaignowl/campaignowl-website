Campaign Owl vs Optmyzr

Facts checked on 27 August 2026 from each company's public site. If something's out of date, tell me and I'll fix it.

Optmyzr is the long-established PPC management suite from ex-Google founders, with a deep library of one-click optimisations, scripts and rule engines. Campaign Owl is narrower and newer: a UK operations platform for agencies and freelancers that puts audits, daily tasks, client comms, tracking and account building in one place. Pick Optmyzr if…

  • You want a very large toolbox and enjoy building rules and scripts.
  • You manage Microsoft or Amazon Ads alongside Google.
  • You want automation that changes bids and budgets on your behalf.
  • You have a team who will invest time learning a big platform.

Pick Campaign Owl if…

  • You want to open one screen on Monday and know what to do across every account — not a toolbox to assemble.
  • Client-facing output matters: white-label reports, activity reports, and written answers to client questions.
  • You want recommendations you approve, with an outcome ledger, rather than automation you have to police.
  • You'd rather have a founder who runs accounts answer your email than a support queue.

Both tools offer a trial with no card, so the honest advice is: run the same account through both for a fortnight and see which one changes what you do on a Monday morning.
