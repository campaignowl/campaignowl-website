Campaign Owl vs TrueClicks

Facts checked on 27 August 2026 from each company's public site. If something's out of date, tell me and I'll fix it.

Campaign Owl is a UK PPC operations platform built by a working account manager: audits with £-at-risk, a daily task rota, client comms, tracking setup and an account builder. Pick TrueClicks if…

  • You spend under $50k/month and want a serious audit tool for free.
  • You need Microsoft Ads in the same view, even read-only.
  • You want the tool to apply fixes directly in Google Ads.
  • Awards and 150+ reviews matter to your buying process.

Pick Campaign Owl if…

  • You run a client book and need white-label reports, client-facing updates and a task rota — not just audit findings.
  • You want the "why did conversions drop?" email written from the data, not a dashboard to interpret.
  • Tracking setup is part of your job: the GTM generator and site scanner save the hours the audit only points at.
  • You build new accounts regularly and want a full Editor-ready build from a URL and a budget.
  • You'd rather pay a flat UK price per account than a percentage of spend as clients grow.

Both tools offer a trial with no card, so the honest advice is: run the same account through both for a fortnight and see which one changes what you do on a Monday morning.
