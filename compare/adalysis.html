Campaign Owl vs Adalysis

Facts checked on 27 August 2026 from each company's public site. If something's out of date, tell me and I'll fix it.

Adalysis is an audit-first PPC tool with letter grades, 100+ prebuilt alerts and a free Google Ads audit report before you sign up. Campaign Owl shares the audit-first DNA — severity-scored findings with the monthly £ at risk — and adds the operations layer around it: tasks, client comms, tracking setup and an account builder. Pick Adalysis if…

  • You want daily automated audits with a huge library of alerts you can customise.
  • You need Microsoft Ads covered.
  • Audit depth is the whole job and you already have your own reporting and task system.

Pick Campaign Owl if…

  • You want the audit to turn into a plan: findings become tasks on a rota, with a client-safe report at the end.
  • Your clients ask "why?" and you want the answer drafted from the data.
  • Tracking setup and new account builds are part of what you sell.
  • You want every finding priced in £ per month so triage is by money, not by category.

Both tools offer a trial with no card, so the honest advice is: run the same account through both for a fortnight and see which one changes what you do on a Monday morning.
