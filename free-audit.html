Questions

Before you connect

Do I need to sign up or enter a card?

No. You sign in with Google to let the audit read your account, that's all. If you want Campaign Owl to keep watching the account, there's a 14-day trial afterwards with no card needed.

I manage client accounts through an MCC. Does it work?

Yes. Sign in with the manager login and pick the client account you want audited. One account per run.

Will anything change in my account?

No. The audit is read-only. It reports and recommends; every change is yours to make.

How long do I have the results?

Seven days from the run, at a private link. You can email the results to yourself or a colleague from the results page.

What if the account is brand new or has very little data?

The audit needs 30 days of activity to say anything useful. On a very new account some checks will report that there isn't enough data yet rather than guess.

Who built this?

An agency PPC with 18 years of Google Ads behind her, who built Campaign Owl to run her own client accounts. The checks are the ones she runs by hand.
