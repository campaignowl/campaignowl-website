This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and service.

What data we collect

When you contact us via email, we collect your email address and the content of your message. When you use the CampaignOwl service, we access your advertising account data through official APIs provided by Google Ads and Microsoft Ads.

The advertising data we access includes:

  • Campaign, ad group, and keyword performance metrics
  • Search term reports
  • Budget and spend data
  • Conversion tracking configuration
  • Account structure and settings
  • Change history

How we use your data

We use your advertising data solely to provide the CampaignOwl service — specifically to audit your accounts, identify issues and opportunities, generate health scores and recommendations, and apply optimisations that you approve.

We do not sell, rent, or share your advertising data with any third parties. Your data is used exclusively to provide the service to you.

Google APIs — Limited Use Disclosure

If you choose to connect Google Tag Manager, we also access your Tag Manager container configuration (tags, triggers and variables) via the Google Tag Manager API — solely to read your existing setup and to create the tags and triggers you explicitly ask Campaign Owl to create. We never modify or delete your existing tags, never publish changes without your confirmation, and never access any data beyond container configuration. You can revoke this access at any time from your Google account security settings.

CampaignOwl's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, CampaignOwl:

  • Only uses Google Ads data to provide and improve the CampaignOwl service for you
  • Does not transfer Google Ads data to third parties unless necessary to provide the service, required by law, or with your explicit consent
  • Does not use Google Ads data for serving advertisements
  • Does not allow humans to read your Google Ads data unless you provide affirmative consent, it is necessary for security purposes, or it is required by law

Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We use secure cloud infrastructure with access controls, encryption at rest and in transit, and regular security reviews.

We retain your advertising data only for as long as your account is active. When you disconnect your advertising accounts or delete your CampaignOwl account, we delete your stored advertising data within 30 days.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Disconnect your advertising accounts at any time

Cookies

Our website uses one cookie automatically: _GRECAPTCHA, set by Google reCAPTCHA on the contact form. This is a security cookie that prevents spam and bot submissions. Under UK PECR and ICO guidance, security cookies are classed as “strictly necessary” and are exempt from the consent requirement — meaning we can set this cookie without asking, but we still disclose it here.

This marketing site uses Google Analytics 4 in Google’s Consent Mode. Until you accept via the cookie banner, the Google tag operates in a cookieless mode: it sets no cookies and sends only anonymous, aggregated pings (no identifiers that track you across visits). If you click Accept, the tag is upgraded and standard GA4 cookies (_ga and similar) are set to measure visits and, where you arrived via one of our ads, attribute sign-ups to the campaign. If you reject or ignore the banner, no analytics or advertising cookies are ever set. We run no Facebook Pixel, no LinkedIn Insight Tag, and no session replay tools.

The Campaign Owl app itself (at app.campaignowl.com) does use Google Analytics 4 and Vercel Analytics. Those are governed by a separate cookie consent banner inside the app and default to denied until you accept.

To withdraw consent to reCAPTCHA, avoid the contact form (email us directly instead) or block reCAPTCHA cookies in your browser settings.
